# Grinfinity visual overhaul

The original menu palette is the reference; the menu itself now matches the new interface. Warm orange (#F5A451), berry (#AB4564), cream (#FFF0CE), plum ink (#321E3D), muted lavender and teal support colours. Rounded, hand-cut silhouettes, thick plum outlines, small offset shadows, controlled highlights. No military gun, neon dashboard or plain geometric boss placeholders.

Gameplay: smiling orange planet with independent face and articulated toy blaster. Blinking eyes, recoil, elastic reactions. Enemy silhouettes communicate behaviour. Bosses become a wind-up cosmic spinner, a cratered brood creature, and a hungry eclipse. Friendly shots are fat cream/gold circles matched to the muzzle; hostile shots are coral diamonds with dark outlines.

Background: layered illustrated nebula ribbons and faint distant planets, with lower contrast than gameplay. The main menu shares this background.

UI: cream lettering, berry cards, orange primary actions, rounded corners and offset shadows. Score top-left, compact wave/time top-right, icon-based effects and abilities along the bottom. One modal at a time: upgrade choice pauses the world, Escape can open pause above it, closing pause restores the choice. No overlapping announcements or live countdown pressure. Results prioritize score and Retry.

Animation: short elastic entrances, responsive gun recoil, gentle character bob/blink, clear hostile anticipation, small particles and controllable shake. The planet body and face rotate with aim. Its rings remain on a stable orbital plane. Shield protection uses a circular translucent bubble; armour uses a plate rather than an unexplained arc.

Use straightforward arcade language. No smile/fighting tagline, smile-themed upgrade names, or suggestion that projectiles are smiles.
